Privacy Policy

Milky Mart · Last updated: 21 July 2026

This policy explains what Milky Mart ("we", "the app") collects when you order dairy products through our Android app, why we collect it, and the control you have over it. We only collect what the service actually needs to work.

Information we collect

  • Mobile number — used to create your account and sign you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Name — shown on your profile and to the delivery partner bringing your order.
  • Delivery addresses — the addresses you save, so we can deliver to them.
  • Order and wallet history — what you ordered, what it cost, and your wallet balance and transactions.
  • Device push token — only if you allow notifications, so we can tell you when your order status changes.
  • Basic technical logs — request timing and error information used to keep the service reliable.
We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data from other apps. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How we use it

  • To take, deliver and track your orders.
  • To operate your wallet and record payments.
  • To notify you about your order (confirmed, packed, out for delivery, delivered).
  • To assign a delivery partner to your address and share only the details needed to complete delivery — your name, address and phone number.
  • To detect abuse and keep accounts secure.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that make the app work, and only what's necessary:

  • Your delivery partner — your name, delivery address and phone number, so the order can be delivered.
  • SMS provider — your mobile number, to send your OTP.
  • Payment gateway — payment details, handled entirely by the gateway. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank credentials.
  • Hosting and error-monitoring providers — to run the service and diagnose faults.

We may also disclose information if legally required to do so.

How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept while your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal, tax or accounting obligations. Push tokens are deleted when you log out or disable notifications. One-time passwords are deleted as soon as they are used or expire (within minutes).

Security

Traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords and one-time codes are stored hashed, never in plain text. Access to the production database is restricted, and sessions can be revoked immediately when you log out.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and its data. Write to the address below and we'll action it within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your profile, addresses and push tokens; order records may be retained where the law requires it.

Children

Milky Mart is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update this page and the "last updated" date above, and notify you in the app where appropriate.
